## 2. The problem  (0:07–0:22)
*On screen:* bullets slide

The problem. A call, a message, a link. Connect your wallet. Install this app. Something got signed, and now someone asks: what happened? Long guides are hard under stress, and the wrong help asks for a seed phrase.

## 3. The solution: Iris  (0:22–0:38)
*On screen:* phone slide with real screenshot m-top.png

The solution is Iris, a free, phone-first page. Paste a public hash and see what was actually signed. Walk a checklist for unknown devices and apps. Or tap Connect and talk, and ask out loud. Iris never asks for keys.

## 4. Why Solana  (0:38–0:56)
*On screen:* phone slide with real screenshot m-chain-approve.png

Why Solana? One signature can carry many instructions behind one tap: transfers, an unlimited approval, a SetAuthority change. Solana's public RPC returns parsed instructions and exact balances, so a phone reads it directly, and Iris turns it into plain lines.

## 5. Built today · open source  (0:56–1:14)
*On screen:* bullets slide

Built today, open source: phone checklists, a chain read for Solana, Bitcoin and Ethereum, offline Ask IRIS in six languages, a Solana decoder with fifty-four passing tests on real mainnet fixtures, and a voice desk on AssemblyAI that refuses secrets.

## 6. How we build  (1:14–1:23)
